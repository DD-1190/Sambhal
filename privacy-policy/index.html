Sambhal Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

Sambhal ("we", "us") sets up and runs automations for small businesses in India, such as replying to customer enquiries on WhatsApp, booking appointments and sending reminders. Our website is sambhalhub.in.

This policy covers two groups of people:

  • Our clients: the businesses that sign up for Sambhal, and the people who work for them.
  • Our clients' customers: anyone who sends a message to a WhatsApp number that Sambhal runs for a client, for example a buyer asking a property dealer about a flat.

For our clients' customers, the client decides why their data is used and is responsible for it. Sambhal processes that data on the client's behalf and only to provide the service they asked for. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the client is the Data Fiduciary and Sambhal is a Data Processor.

2. Information we collect

From our clients:

  • Business name, city, contact person, phone number and email.
  • Business data the client gives us to run their automations, such as property listings, price lists, appointment slots and customer lists.
  • WhatsApp Business account details the client connects, such as the phone number ID and access token.
  • Billing records, and notes of the work we do for them.

From our clients' customers, when they message a client on WhatsApp:

  • WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
  • The messages they send and the replies sent to them.
  • Choices they make in the chat, such as property type, budget, preferred area and site visit time.
  • Booking details, such as the date and time of a site visit or appointment.

We do not collect payment card details, government ID numbers or location data from customers. We do not use cookies or trackers on the Sambhal portal beyond a single sign-in cookie.

3. How we use information

We use information only to provide and improve the service a client has asked for:

  • Reply to customer enquiries on the client's WhatsApp number, share matching listings or products, and book visits or appointments.
  • Send reminders and updates the customer has asked for or would expect, such as a reminder before a booked visit.
  • Alert the client about new enquiries and bookings.
  • Prepare monthly reports for the client, and track usage so we can bill WhatsApp and AI costs at cost.
  • Find and fix errors, and keep the service secure.

Automated and AI replies. Many replies are sent automatically. When a customer types a question in their own words, we may use an AI model to draft an answer from the client's own information, such as their listings. A customer can ask to speak to a person at any time, and the chat is handed to the client.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for our own advertising. We do not use our clients' customer data to train AI models.

5. Storage, security and retention

Each client's data is kept separate from every other client's. Access to the portal is password-protected, data travels over encrypted connections, and only Sambhal staff who run a client's automations can see that client's data.

How long we keep information:

  • Customer chats and bookings: for as long as the client uses Sambhal, then deleted within 90 days of the client leaving, unless the client asks us to delete them sooner.
  • Client account and billing records: for as long as the account is open, and afterwards for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires.
  • Error logs: up to 90 days.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects personal data, we will inform the affected client and, where the law requires, the Data Protection Board of India.

6. Your rights and how to delete your data

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. You can also withdraw consent to receive messages at any time.

To stop messages: reply STOP in the WhatsApp chat, or block the number.

To delete your data:

  1. Send a message to Sambhal on WhatsApp at +91 98734 70074, with the subject "Delete my data".
  2. Include the WhatsApp number you used and, if you know it, the name of the business you messaged.
  3. We will confirm your request within 7 days and delete your chats and bookings within 30 days. We will tell you when it is done.

If you are a client's customer, you may also ask that business directly; they can ask us to delete your data at any time.

7. Children, changes and contact

Children. Sambhal is a service for businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. If you think a child has shared data with us, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes. We may update this policy as Sambhal grows. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will tell our clients before it takes effect.
