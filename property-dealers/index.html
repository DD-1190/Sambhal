FAQ

Questions dealers ask

How many leads will I get?

We won’t promise a number — it depends on your area, your listings and your ad budget. You’ll see leads, visits and cost per visit every week, and you can change the budget or stop anytime.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly to Meta from an ad account in your name. Our fee covers making the ads, running them and tuning them every week.

Can I stop the ads and keep the secretary?

Yes. Drop back to the ₹15,000 Secretary plan any month.

Will buyers know it’s not me?

Messages go from your number, in your name and your tone. Anything serious — negotiation, a hot buyer, a complaint — comes straight to you.

Where does my lead data go?

Into your own Google Sheet. It is never shared with portals or other dealers, and you can take it with you anytime.

Will it quote the wrong price?

It only shares prices from your listing sheet. Discounts and negotiation always come to you.

Does it work with 99acres, MagicBricks and Housing.com?

Yes. We connect the lead alerts you already get from them, plus your WhatsApp and Facebook leads.

Can it talk in Hindi?

Yes — Hindi, English or Hinglish, whichever the buyer writes in.

What about RERA?

Your RERA registration number appears on every listing card. It doesn’t give legal advice — those questions come to you.
